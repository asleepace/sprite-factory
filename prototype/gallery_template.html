Sprite Factory Sampler

100 learning-game nouns, authored as shapes by an LLM and rendered by one fixed style rig. Every pixel, shade and outline comes from the factory, not the author.

Locked palette

24 materials × 5 shades. Authors pick a material; the light direction decides the shade. Shadows drift toward blue-violet and highlights toward yellow, which is what keeps 100 sprites looking like one set.
